Clock Sucking Simulator - Game Idea

Brief idea of the game:
- You work at a shitty company which sucks the life out of you, so you decide to suck the life out of the clock.
- But you can't get caught by your boss or your co-workers who would snitch on you (to the boss) the first opportunity they get.
- Your goal is to waste the most amount of time (by not actually doing your job) but also not be fired for doing less 
  work than your co-workers.
- You have one 6-day work week to beat your co-workers and your boss and become employee of the week.

Game mechanics:
- This is a multiplayer game where one person becomes the boss (randomly) and others become employees.
- Goal of the employees is to maximize their utility by the end of the week.
- Goal of the boss is to catch any slacking employees, if he is able to fire all bad employees by the end of the week
  boss wins. There is a work threshold which gets completed by the empployees then also the boss wins.
- If the days work is not completed the worst employee of the day gets eliminated (fired) and boss gets points.

Player mechanics (employees):
- Employees can see the cabins of all the co-workers and the position of the boss always.
- Mode 1: Honest work - employee works till the mode changes.
- Mode 2: Clock sucking - employee wastes time by not doing work and chilling (or snitching).
- Mode 3: Snitch - employee can select a co-worker to snitch on them.
          Employee will go to the cabin of the other employee to collect evidence, 
          if they find the other employee clock-sucking they have evidence, 
          otherwise the other employee has the option of collecting evidence against this employee (for roaming around).
- Time spent in Mode 3 (Snitch) will also be counted as clock-sucking and be rewarded similarly.
- If an employee has collected evidence against a co-worker (he can report them to the boss when he comes to their cabin and finds them working).
- If an employee has evidence against another employee but get caught themselves they have the option of getting eliminated or
  snitching on the employee (this will reset their work done but save them from getting fired). However if the work threshold is incomplete
  and they are the lowest working employee of the day they still get fired.
- Employee can switch instantly from Mode-2 or Mode-3 to Mode-1 if they are inside their own cabin.

Player mechanics (boss):
- Boss roams the corridor and cannot see inside a cabin without entering it.
- Boss can instantly fire an employee not working (even if he finds them roaming in corridor).
- If a player has evidence against another they will still be fired first if caugt clock-sucking.
- At the end of the day boss can choose to fire the lowest working employee if the days work is not completed.
  However the boss can choose not to eliminate them.
- Entering a room would not be instant (maybe a 0.25 second delay).

Point system (for employees):
- A day consists of 8 work hours and 1 hour of lunch break (1 hour translates to 15 seconds of game time).
- Every hour of honest work gains 1 utility point and completes 1 unit of work.
- Every hour of clock-sucking gains 5 utility points and completes 0 units of work.

Point system (for boss):
- Getting the days work done by the employees gains 2 utility points.
- Catching a clock-sucker gains the boss 5 utility points.

Map design (initial idea):
- There are 4 cabins in a sqare like grid with 2 gates to every cabin (any player can enter or leave from here any time).
- There is a middle corridor in the office where boss patrols and tries to catch a clock-sucker.
- Later there can be custom maps containing more cabins (lets keep the office a regular polygon for now for any size).

Gotchas and other ideas to explore:
- The timings and point system described above is for example and not the final thing (it will need to be fine tuned for maximum fun).
- How about adding a delay for switching between modes for employees.
